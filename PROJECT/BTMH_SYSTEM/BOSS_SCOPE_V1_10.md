# Boss Scope – V1.10

V1.10 remains Version 1 of the static-camera module:
- Local/offline runtime after installation.
- Student management + 2-pass FaceID registration.
- Realtime recognition + liveness-gated check-in.
- Classroom tracking + simple actions.
- Enterprise History dashboard.
- Attendance sessions and reports.
- Camera configuration, System Health, backup/restore and local minimal RBAC.
- Easy Windows deployment entry points and installer builder.

Not in V1.10:
- Real PTZ control.
- Automatic camera pan/tilt/zoom to the speaker.
- Claims of IR/depth-grade anti-spoof security.

PTZ remains V2 after hardware/model details are available.
